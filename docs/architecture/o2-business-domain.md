# Business Domains

## Overview

The Enterprise Healthcare Integration Platform is divided into business domains. Each domain is responsible for a specific area of hospital operations and owns its business rules and data.

---

## Authentication

Responsibilities:

- User authentication
- JWT token management
- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

---

## Patient Management

Responsibilities:

- Patient registration
- Medical Record Number generation
- Patient demographics
- Patient search
- Patient history

---

## Laboratory

Responsibilities:

- Laboratory orders
- Laboratory requests
- Laboratory results
- LIS integration

---

## Pharmacy

Responsibilities:

- Medication management
- Dispensing
- Inventory management

---

## Billing

Responsibilities:

- Invoice generation
- Payment recording
- Insurance claims

---

## Reporting

Responsibilities:

- Operational reports
- Ministry of Health reporting
- Dashboard metrics

---

## Administration

Responsibilities:

- User management
- Role management
- Department configuration

---

## Integration

Responsibilities:

- FHIR APIs
- HL7 messaging
- ERP integration
- Insurance integration
- External EMR/HMIS integration
